## Scope of extraction

What this project took from the wiki, stated plainly so that anyone can judge the extent of reuse:

| Item | Taken? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Article **text** | Yes | Read from roughly 120 article pages plus the 17 yearly timeline pages, then condensed, translated into Chinese and restructured by topic |
| Article **images, flags, portraits, maps** | **No** | Not downloaded, not referenced, not embedded anywhere in this repository |
| Audio / video | **No** | — |
| Wiki templates, infoboxes as-is | **No** | Data points were extracted and rewritten as prose or tables |
| Verbatim English passages | **No** | None retained; the knowledge base is Chinese-language rewriting, not reproduction |

Pages were read **one at a time** through a standard web-fetch tool, at a deliberately low rate, over a single collection session. No bulk-download infrastructure was built, and no wiki account, API key or database dump was used.

## Translation caveat

The Chinese text is **not** an official translation. It condenses, restructures and
occasionally annotates the source. Where the source wiki is self-contradictory, both
readings are preserved and flagged (see `references/15-factcheck-standards.md` §7)
rather than silently reconciled. For authoritative wording, consult the wiki itself.
