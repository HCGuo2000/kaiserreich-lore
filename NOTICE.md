## Translation caveat

The Chinese text is **not** an official translation. It condenses, restructures and
occasionally annotates the source. Where the source wiki is self-contradictory, both
readings are preserved and flagged (see `references/15-factcheck-standards.md` §7)
rather than silently reconciled. For authoritative wording, consult the wiki itself.
